// 01 — SERVICES

Full-scope electrical work

From a single dead outlet to a full panel replacement — one call covers it.

Panel Upgrades & Service

Replace outdated fuse boxes and maxed-out panels with modern 100–200 amp service. Safer, room to grow, and ready for EVs and additions.

Lighting Installation

Recessed cans, pendants, under-cabinet, landscape and security lighting — placed right, dimmed right, wired clean.

Troubleshooting & Repairs

Breakers that trip, outlets that died, lights that flicker. We find the actual fault fast and fix it — not just the symptom.
